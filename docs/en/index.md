## Catalogs 

To navigate, use the menu on the left and explore the different datasets organized according to their use options, which may be:  

- **Download** and programmatic access with [LSDB](https://lsdb.io/) (data in HATS format)  
- **Query** in databases, through the platforms [User Query](https://userquery.linea.org.br/), [Jupyter Hub](https://jupyter.linea.org.br/), or [TAP Service](https://userquery.linea.org.br/cms/services/scripted-access/)  
- **Processing** in the HPC environment of data stored in the Lustre system, accessible through the [Open OnDemand](https://ondemand.linea.org.br) platform
