# LSST DP2 FITS products

The **Legacy Survey of Space and Time Data Preview 2 (LSST DP2)** contains image and catalog products generated by Rubin Science Pipelines v30.0.8 from LSSTCam observations obtained between April 2025 and January 2026. Early DP2 was released on July 27, 2026. In this phase, the published image product is the deep coadd. Visit and difference images are part of the full release, expected in October–December 2026.

- [Official DP2 documentation](https://dp2.lsst.io/)
- [DP2 data products](https://dp2.lsst.io/products/index.html)
- [Official dataset citation](https://doi.org/10.71929/rubin/3382528)

## Sky coverage by band

Deep coadds cover about 3,000 square degrees in the Wide-Fast-Deep, Small Field Survey, and Deep Drilling Field regions. Individual visits cover about 15,000 square degrees, but those images are not part of this copy.

The local copy contains 197,105 patches in 2,191 tracts, with identifiers between 2054 and 13041. Declination for these patches runs from −52.16° to +22.90°, and right ascension spans the full circle. Not every patch exists in every band; the table below gives the count per filter.

!!! info "How to read the coverage"
    Each file is one skymap patch. Deep coadds include only exposures that meet the quality criteria; edge patches can contain regions with no data, flagged by the `NO_DATA` mask.

Official sources: [DP2 documentation](https://dp2.lsst.io/), [deep coadd](https://dp2.lsst.io/products/images/deep_coadd.html), and [image coaddition](https://dp2.lsst.io/processing/coaddition/index.html).

### Deep coadd

The combination, per band and patch, of calibrated, background-subtracted exposures. Stacking uses cell-based coaddition with an inverse-variance weighted mean. Each file is a `CellCoadd` in one of the six bands `u`, `g`, `r`, `i`, `z`, and `y`.

- **Format:** FITS (`.fits`)
- **Local inventory:** 925,460 files, 27.71 TB
- **Contents:** image (nJy), variance (nJy²), and mask planes, plus PSF, photometric calibration, and WCS
- **Official documentation:** [Deep coadd](https://dp2.lsst.io/products/images/deep_coadd.html) and [image coaddition](https://dp2.lsst.io/processing/coaddition/index.html)

## Limits of this inventory

This page describes only the deep coadds in the local LIneA copy. This set does not include `deep_coadd_background`, `deep_coadd_n_image`, or HealSparse maps. Official Early DP2 also limits image products to deep coadds. Raw exposures, visit images, template coadds, and difference images are not registered as available in this copy. See the [DP2 product index](https://dp2.lsst.io/products/index.html).
